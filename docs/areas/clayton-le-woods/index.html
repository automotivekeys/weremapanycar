Questions

Is remapping safe for my engine?

Yes, when it's done properly. We stay within the safe limits of your standard turbo, injectors and clutch, and we check the vehicle is healthy before we start.

Do I need to tell my insurer?

Yes. A remap is a modification, so let your insurer know. Many cover it for little or no extra cost.

Can it be put back to standard?

Yes. We keep a copy of your original settings, and putting them back is free if you sell the vehicle.

Do I need to bring it to a garage?

No. We're fully mobile. All we need is a safe place to park, like your drive or the car park at work.
