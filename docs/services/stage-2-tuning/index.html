Stage 2 & Custom Tuning

Calibration built around your upgraded hardware, checked with datalogs.

Time 2 to 4 hoursFor upgraded intake, intercooler, exhaust or turboMobile · we come to you

Stage 2 is for vehicles with supporting modifications: a bigger intercooler, uprated intake, downpipe, hybrid turbo or uprated fuelling. The calibration is written around that hardware, then refined with datalogs of boost, air mass, rail pressure and exhaust gas temperature.

We will be honest about where the limits are. If your clutch or gearbox will not hold the torque, we tell you before we tune, not after.

What's included

  • Hardware check before tuning
  • Logged runs before and after
  • Revisions included for 30 days
  • Gearbox torque limits matched

Questions

Will a remap damage my engine?

A good remap stays inside the limits of the standard turbo, injectors and clutch, and we check the vehicle is healthy before we start. Problems usually come from tuning a vehicle that already has a fault, or from generic files pushed too far. We do neither.

Do I need to tell my insurer?

Yes. A remap is a modification, and most insurers will ask about it. Many will cover it for a small extra premium or none at all. Not declaring it can invalidate your cover.

Can you put it back to standard?

Yes. We keep your original file, and putting it back is free if you sell the vehicle or need it for a dealer visit.

Will it affect my warranty?

A manufacturer can refuse a claim on parts related to the change. Some ECUs record that they have been reprogrammed even after the original file goes back. If your vehicle is under warranty, we'll talk it through before you decide.
